Architectural Highlights

PR-AUC Model Governance
At ~0.5% fraud prevalence, ROC-AUC gives false confidence. PR-AUC directly isolates true positives vs false alerts.
Two-Layer Telemetry
Layer A tracks rolling drift without ground truth; Layer B evaluates delayed chargeback feedback against hard thresholds.
Stationarity & PSI
Empirically tracks monthly Population Stability Index (< 0.10) and KS test across features to ensure data continuity.
Zero PII Leakage
Customer credit card numbers, names, and street addresses are dropped prior to transformation.
