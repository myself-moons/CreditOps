What CreditOps Monitors

Credit card fraud presents extreme class imbalance (~0.33% to 0.58%). Standard accuracy is misleading. CreditOps employs a rigorous two-layer telemetry architecture to distinguish between operational drift and model degradation.

Layer A • Operational Telemetry

Real-Time Zero Ground-Truth Monitoring

Evaluated continuously at inference time directly from production logs (predictions.jsonl). No customer dispute outcomes are required.

  • Rolling Fraud Detection Rate: Compares window rate vs 0.579% training baseline (tolerance: ±0.003).
  • Probability Distribution: Tracks mean score, spread, and standard deviation to detect model calibration drift.
  • Population Stability Index (PSI): Binned distribution drift for continuous features (log_amt, hour, dist_to_merch). Threshold: < 0.10.
  • Inference Latency SLA: Tracks mean and 95th-percentile response times to detect system bottlenecks.
Layer B • Performance & Retrain Governance

Delayed Ground-Truth Evaluation

Evaluated periodically when actual customer disputes and confirmed fraud chargebacks become available (typically 14–60 days post-transaction).

  • Precision-Recall AUC (PR-AUC): Primary selection metric. Drops below 0.10 trigger an automated retraining alert.
  • Recall at 5% FP Budget: Measures fraud capture rate strictly bounded by a 5% false-positive customer friction budget. Drops below 0.60 flag degradation.
  • Subgroup Parity: Flags fairness or blind-spots across merchant categories, transaction buckets, and age bands.
  • Automated Retrain Trigger: Evaluated in retrain_trigger.py to govern champion-challenger promotion.
